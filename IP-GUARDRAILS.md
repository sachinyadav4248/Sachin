# IP Guardrails

Sizzle Street is a genre entrant, not a clone of any specific product. This file is the
standing rule set. It applies to every contributor, human or agent, and to every commit.

## The line

Game mechanics, systems and mathematics are not protectable expression. Names, logos, art,
audio, text and screenshots are. The project may replicate the former freely and must
originate the latter entirely.

### Safe to replicate

- The idle-arcade loop: serve, earn, upgrade, expand, idle income.
- Dual-channel serving, station chains, carry stacks, patience timers, staff automation.
- Economy mathematics: geometric upgrade costs, multiplicative income, capped offline
  accrual, prestige curves. Formulas are ideas, not expression.
- Monetisation structure: rewarded doublers, time-skips, no-ads IAP, season passes.
- Broad art direction: low-poly stylised 3D, bright saturated palettes, tilted top-down
  cameras. A style is not ownable; a specific model is.

### Never permitted

- The name, logo, wordmark or icon of any existing game — including "Pizza Ready" and any
  Supercent or Molip mark.
- Any art, model, texture, animation, sound, music or UI asset originating from another
  game, ripped, traced or "redrawn from".
- Store copy, screenshots, feature graphics or video captured from another product.
- Real-world brand references, including parodies and near-misses of real restaurant,
  vehicle or product brands. Every brand that appears in-world is invented.
- Character designs that read as a specific existing game's mascot. Our chef is defined in
  [`docs/05-content-pipeline.md`](docs/05-content-pipeline.md) and must not converge on
  another game's silhouette or colour signature.

## Checks before adding an asset

Every asset added to the repository requires all four:

1. **Provenance.** Where it came from, in the commit message. Original work, commissioned
   work, or a licensed pack named by version.
2. **Licence.** Commercial use permitted, redistribution within a compiled binary
   permitted. Recorded as a row in [`ATTRIBUTIONS.md`](ATTRIBUTIONS.md), in the same commit.
3. **Reverse image / audio search** for anything that might be derivative.
4. **Named owner** who confirms the check was done.

Assets generated by an AI tool are not exempt. Record the tool, the prompt, and confirm the
tool's terms grant commercial rights to the output. A prompt that names an existing game,
studio or character is prohibited — it is the single most likely way to produce infringing
output by accident.

## Store listing

Written from scratch. Keyword overlap with competitors is expected and fine — idle, tycoon,
restaurant, cooking, simulator are descriptive terms. Copied sentences are not. Never name a
competitor in the listing, and never use a competitor's screenshot in a comparison.

## Standing prohibitions for agents working in this repository

- Do not fetch, copy or paraphrase another game's store listing into project files.
- Do not download assets from asset-rip sites, mod repositories or game data dumps.
- Do not name a real game, studio or character in an asset-generation prompt.
- If a task appears to require any of the above, stop and escalate rather than improvising.

## If a claim arrives

Preserve everything, change nothing, escalate to counsel immediately. Do not remove the
allegedly infringing asset before it is documented, and do not respond directly.
